How can we help?

Answers for account access, photo animation, purchases and privacy in FrameMorrow.

Generation issues

Clear, unobstructed faces usually produce the most reliable results. If FrameMorrow's quality check detects an unexpected person, significant identity drift or another unsafe result, the video is rejected instead of being released.

Free preview and export unlock

Eligible accounts can create one free animation preview. You can watch it in FrameMorrow and keep it in Memories on your device. To save the existing preview to Photos or share it, choose Save to Gallery or Share and confirm an unlock for 1 credit. Unlocking does not regenerate the video; once unlocked, saving and sharing are available without spending another credit for that video.

Purchases and credits

Credits purchased through the App Store are verified by FrameMorrow's backend before being granted. A transaction is designed to grant credits only once. If a purchase appears incomplete, do not repurchase immediately; allow StoreKit to redeliver the transaction after service recovery.

Delete your account

Open Account → Delete account permanently in the app. This removes the FrameMorrow account and server-side account data, then removes memories stored inside FrameMorrow on that device. Copies already exported to Photos or another app remain under your control.

Privacy

Read the full FrameMorrow Privacy Policy for information about photo processing, temporary AI-provider handling, purchases and retention.

Contact support

For questions about your account, animation results, purchases, credits or privacy, email fenixlabgames@gmail.com.

When contacting us, describe the issue and your app version. Never email passwords, private keys, full payment details, or private family photographs.
