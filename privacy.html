Privacy Policy

FrameMorrow helps turn still photographs into short animated memories. This policy explains what data is processed, why it is needed, and how long it is kept.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Account data

FrameMorrow uses Firebase Authentication. When you create an account, Firebase processes authentication information such as your email address and account identifier. FrameMorrow also stores server-side account state needed to operate free generations, credits, generation jobs and verified purchase entitlements.

Photographs and generated videos

A photograph stays on your device until you choose to start a generation. When you start, the selected photograph is sent over HTTPS to FrameMorrow's backend and to configured AI processing providers, including fal.ai and PixVerse, to create the requested animation.

FrameMorrow does not use uploaded photographs to identify or name people and does not perform face recognition. Face visibility and geometry may be analyzed only to choose safer motion instructions and reduce identity changes, unexpected people and other generation artifacts.

Quality assurance

Before a generated animation is released, FrameMorrow compares the source image with sampled video frames. A reduced reference image may be kept temporarily so this check survives a backend restart. The reference expires after at most two hours and is cleared earlier after validation when possible. The remaining temporary verdict contains machine-readable pass/fail flags rather than a free-text description of the photograph.

Local storage and export

Completed memories are stored locally inside FrameMorrow on the device. If you choose Save to Photos, a copy is written to the system photo library. If you choose Share, the operating system's share interface is used. Copies exported outside FrameMorrow are controlled by you and the selected destination.

Service providers

  • Firebase / Google — authentication, App Check and account state.
  • Render — FrameMorrow application backend.
  • fal.ai and configured AI model providers such as PixVerse — image-to-video generation and safety/quality processing.
  • Apple — App Store purchases and transaction verification when purchases are enabled.

Purchases

When in-app purchases are enabled, FrameMorrow processes verified App Store transaction identifiers and entitlement information so credits can be granted once and restored consistently. FrameMorrow does not receive full payment-card details.

Tracking and advertising

FrameMorrow does not sell personal data, does not use uploaded photographs for advertising, and does not use personal data for cross-app advertising tracking. The current app does not contain third-party advertising.

Account deletion

A signed-in user can open Account → Delete account permanently. After confirmation, FrameMorrow deletes server-side account data and the Firebase Authentication identity, then removes FrameMorrow memories stored inside the app on that device. Videos already exported to Photos or another service are outside FrameMorrow's app storage and are not automatically deleted.

Retention

  • temporary AI-provider uploads use short-lived provider storage where supported;
  • reduced FrameMorrow QA references expire after at most two hours;
  • account, entitlement and transaction metadata is retained while necessary to operate the account, purchases and abuse protection;
  • local generated videos remain on the device until you delete them, delete the account, clear app data or remove the app.

Security

FrameMorrow uses HTTPS, Firebase Authentication and Firebase App Check. AI provider credentials are held server-side and are not embedded in the mobile application.

Children

FrameMorrow is not designed as a service directed specifically to children. Users are responsible for having the rights or necessary permission to upload a photograph.
